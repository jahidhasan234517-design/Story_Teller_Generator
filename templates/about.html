About

News Intelligence Dashboard

This dashboard collects public, no-auth news stories from Hacker News and Google News, then normalizes, categorizes, scores, and analyzes them using lightweight Python intelligence services.

It is designed to help users monitor trending stories, sentiment, topic frequency, and publication patterns without requiring paid APIs or account setup.

  • Dashboard overview with KPIs
  • Search and combined filters
  • Trending and topic intelligence
  • Analytics powered by Chart.js
  • Bookmarking for saved story tracking
